GOOD TO KNOW / 05

Questions,
answered.

Can I buy Aurora now?+

Not yet. Checkout and license activation are still being prepared. This page will link to the live purchase flow when sales open.

What can the current studio do?+

The local MVP can import video files, use a compatible AI service to help select moments, render vertical clips, and let you preview and download the results. Some editing and automation features are still in development.

Do I need my own AI account?+

Yes, the current studio uses a customer-provided compatible AI endpoint and key. Usage charges from that provider are separate.

Can I use any video?+

Use footage you own or are authorized to process. You remain responsible for the rights and platform rules that apply to your content.
