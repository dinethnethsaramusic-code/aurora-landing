01 / WHY CHANGE

The recording is done.
The clipping work isn't.

Finding the right moment, reframing it, writing a hook, and captioning every word takes time. A subscription or credit meter can make every extra clip feel like another charge.

Aurora Clips gives you a local workflow for turning the footage you already own into shorts worth reviewing.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

From file to finished clips in three steps.

You decide what to keep. Aurora handles the repetitive parts of preparing options.

  1. 01

    Bring a video file

    Start with a local recording you own or are authorized to process. Aurora transcribes speech with word timestamps.

  2. 02

    Review the moments

    AI-assisted selection and scoring surface candidate sections. Choose the moments that fit your audience.

  3. 03

    Make the short clips

    Aurora reframes to 9:16 and burns in captions and hook overlays. It also makes thumbnails and title and description suggestions. Preview and download what you want.

Which plan should I choose?

Will it run on my PC?

Aurora Clips is for Windows 11 PCs. A GPU is optional; processing can take longer without one. Recommended RAM and disk requirements are [TODO: confirm] before launch.

How much will the AI provider cost?

It depends on your provider, model, and usage. You use your own account and pay that provider separately. Aurora does not charge per-minute credits.

Do I need my own AI key?

Yes. Aurora needs an OpenAI-compatible provider key for AI-assisted moment selection. If you want a service with no setup or AI key, Aurora is not the right fit.

How many devices can use one license?

One license is for one person and up to two devices. [TODO: confirm device replacement process.]

What happens after 1.x updates end on the lifetime plan?

All 1.x updates are included with the Lifetime license. The version you bought keeps working after those updates end. A future major version may be a separate purchase.

Can I get a refund?

The Lifetime license has a 14-day refund window from purchase. Yearly and Monthly have a 14-day refund window on the first payment. See the Refund Policy.

Can I clip from YouTube or Twitch?

Use footage you own or are authorized to process. Local files are the core workflow. YouTube, Twitch, and Kick link import is beta and may fail when platforms change; it does not give you rights to someone else's content.

Is there a Mac version?

No. The Windows ZIP is the main route. Docker is an advanced/beta option, not a Mac app.

How is this different from a cloud clipping service?

Aurora runs on your own PC. You can choose a one-time license or a subscription. Aurora does not impose a product minute quota. You handle setup, processing time, storage, and your own AI provider account.

What leaves my machine?

Your source video files stay on your PC. Your API key is stored there and sent directly to your chosen provider for requests. AI analysis also sends selected text and, if enabled, frames. Read the Privacy Policy for more detail.
