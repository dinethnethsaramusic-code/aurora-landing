Can I buy Aurora now?+

Not yet. This page will link to checkout when sales open.

What videos can I use?+

At launch, Aurora accepts local video files you own or are allowed to process. Link import is not included.

Do I need an AI provider key?+

Yes. You choose a compatible provider and use your own key. Its usage charges are separate from Aurora.

What will AI usage cost per video?+

It depends on video length and the provider and model you choose. Check the provider’s current rates. We will publish a measured example before sales open.

How many devices can I use?+

One person can activate a license on two devices. Keys cannot be shared or resold.

Can I get a refund?+

Yes. The planned refund window is 14 days from purchase, with no questions asked. Lemon Squeezy will process refunds. See the Refund Policy.

What happens after 1.x updates end?+

The version you bought keeps working. Compatibility fixes remain free. A future major version may be a separate purchase.
