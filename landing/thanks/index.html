Download and install

  1. Use the download link supplied with your purchase to get AuroraStudio-Windows.zip. [TODO: add the verified licensed-release download link before sales open.]
  2. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder you control, such as Documents. Do not run the app from inside the ZIP.
  3. Open AuroraStudio.exe. Keep its console window open while processing. Aurora opens the local studio in your browser.
  4. Enter your license key on the activation screen, then follow the setup steps for your AI provider and output folder.

Find your license key

Open the Lemon Squeezy receipt email sent to your checkout address and look for the Aurora license key. Check Spam or Promotions if the receipt is missing. Copy the key into Aurora's activation screen.
